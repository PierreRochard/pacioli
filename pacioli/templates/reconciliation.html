Collect from signed-in Firefox

Open Amazon’s Your Orders page, select the history range, and click the Pacioli Local Amazon Reader toolbar button. If its button is hidden, use Firefox menu → Extensions → Pacioli Local Amazon Reader; its badge should say ON. Collection saves encrypted receipts locally. Sign-in or unexpected pages pause for your attention. Review item and settlement evidence below before booking.

Reader missing from Firefox?

Temporary readers must be loaded again after Firefox restarts. Open about:debugging#/runtime/this-firefox, choose Load Temporary Add-on…, and select manifest.json in Pacioli’s extensions/amazon-local folder. In the file picker, Ctrl+L lets you enter the full path. Press Enter to locate the file, then choose Select to load it. Return to Amazon Your Orders and select the reader from Firefox’s Extensions menu.
