Method and scope

  • The direct method reports recorded cash receipts and payments during the inclusive selected dates, grouped by their supported operating, investing, or financing purpose.
  • The consolidated scope is the cash accounts recorded in Pacioli. Transfers between included cash accounts are eliminated when supported; unresolved transfers remain visible for review.
  • Credit-card purchases do not move cash and are excluded at purchase. Payments from cash accounts to cards remain cash outflows; payments without a supported activity allocation remain unclassified.
  • Opening balances and other timing or basis adjustments are reconciled separately. Recorded opening or ending cash may be incomplete where source coverage or opening-balance evidence is missing.
  • Amounts with unresolved purpose stay in Unclassified cash flows. A zero reconciliation difference checks the arithmetic of the recorded scope; it does not certify completeness or the correctness of every classification.
  • Owner-confirmed purposes may support an aggregate report classification while the ledger awaits a detailed allocation. Household employee payments combine confirmed wages, taxes, and fees without asserting their individual amounts. Such rows retain references to the reviewed evidence.
  • Accounting dates use UTC, consistent with Pacioli's bank-feed ledger. The direct-method presentation separates gross cash receipts and payments; see IAS 7's description of the method. This personal report is not a claim of full standards compliance.
