Local Chase sync

Update your saved Chase accounts using your signed-in Firefox and the workstation's local model. Missing intervals and recent dates are checked for missing or late-posted transactions. Each QFX also supplies a posted balance. Existing transactions are kept once. Updates do not post journal entries.

One click updates the saved accounts through today, or resumes an interrupted update. You can leave this Pacioli page while it runs. If Chase needs sign-in or verification, return here after completing it in Firefox and press Update Chase again.

    Balances and opening candidates

    All amounts are USD. The opening candidate is the posted balance minus signed transactions from the start of coverage through the bank's balance date. It is the balance immediately before that starting date. Credit-card balances owed are negative in this calculation.

    AccountPosted balanceBank as-of dateImported coverageSigned activity through balance dateOpening candidateEvidence / review

    A bank balance can predate today's fetch, including on weekends. Available credit and pending transactions are excluded. Coverage is based on QFX ranges and verified export requests; it cannot prove Chase supplied every transaction. Reconcile candidates to statements before posting opening entries.
